F-Series Flat Series Fire Boats and Rescue Boats

HDPE construction

HDPE Fire Boats and Rescue Boats

Tideman Marine constructs its vessels using High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE), providing characteristics that are particularly well suited for professional fire boats and rescue boats.

Emergency response vessels may encounter docks, submerged objects, debris, rocky shorelines, shallow water, saltwater, and other demanding conditions. HDPE provides departments with a rugged hull material designed for these real-world marine environments.

Why HDPE Works for Fire Boats and Rescue Boats

01

Impact Resistance

HDPE provides excellent impact resistance for operations around docks, debris, rocky shorelines, and other demanding environments.

02

Corrosion Resistance

HDPE does not corrode from exposure to freshwater or saltwater, making it well suited for long-term marine service.

03

Low Maintenance

The HDPE hull does not require the same corrosion protection associated with many traditional marine materials.

04

No Electrolysis

HDPE is nonconductive and is not subject to galvanic corrosion.

05

Naturally Buoyant

HDPE itself is naturally buoyant, an important characteristic for a marine hull material.

06

Shallow-Water Capability

HDPE construction works particularly well with rugged, shallow-draft workboat designs such as the F-Series Flat Series.

07

Long Service Life

HDPE is designed to provide years of demanding marine service with relatively low hull maintenance.

08

Repairability

If damage does occur, HDPE can be welded and repaired.

For departments whose rescue boats may encounter submerged debris, damaged docks, rocky shorelines, flood-related hazards, or other challenging conditions, HDPE can provide an important operational advantage.
